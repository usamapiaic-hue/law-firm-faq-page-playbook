# Law Firm FAQ Page Playbook

How to plan, write and mark up FAQ sections on a law firm website so they answer real client questions, support the practice pages, and give search engines and AI assistants a clear answer to quote. Published by [Legal Web Agency](https://legalwebagency.com/).

A good FAQ section does three jobs. It removes the doubts that stop a visitor from calling, it captures the long, specific questions people type into Google, and it gives AI tools a short, quotable answer. A bad one is a page of twenty vague questions nobody asked. For examples, see our guide to [law firm website FAQs](https://legalwebagency.com/law-firm-website-faqs/).

## Step 1: Collect real questions

| Source | What to look for |
|--------|------------------|
| Intake calls | The first three questions callers ask, by practice |
| Consultation notes | Worries about cost, timeline and process |
| Search Console | Queries that start with *how*, *can*, *do I*, *what happens* |
| Google "People also ask" | Questions for your main practice and city |
| Reviews | What clients say surprised or reassured them |

Aim for 5 to 8 questions per practice page, not one giant list.

## Step 2: Put each question where it belongs

- **Practice pages:** questions about that case type, such as deadlines, what the case is worth and how long it takes. See [law firm practice area pages](https://legalwebagency.com/law-firm-practice-area-pages/).
- **Contact page:** questions that stop people from reaching out, such as cost of a consultation, languages spoken and after-hours calls. See [law firm contact page design](https://legalwebagency.com/law-firm-contact-page-design/).
- **About page:** questions about the firm itself. See [the law firm about us page](https://legalwebagency.com/law-firm-about-us-page/).
- **A central FAQ page:** only for general questions that do not fit elsewhere, linking out to the practice pages.

## Step 3: Write answers that can be quoted

1. Answer in the first sentence. *"In most states you have two years to file a personal injury claim, but some deadlines are much shorter."*
2. Add one or two sentences of context.
3. Link to the page with the full explanation.
4. Keep each answer under about 80 words.
5. Avoid promises about results and words like *guaranteed* or *best*. Check your state on our [attorney advertising rules by state](https://legalwebagency.com/attorney-advertising-rules-by-state-law-firm-website/) hub.

Short, direct answers like these are what Google's AI Overviews and chat assistants tend to lift. See [Google AI Overviews for law firms](https://legalwebagency.com/google-ai-overviews-for-law-firms/) and [does ChatGPT recommend your law firm?](https://legalwebagency.com/does-chatgpt-recommend-your-law-firm/).

## Step 4: Mark it up

Add FAQPage structured data only for questions that are visible on the page, with the same wording. Google shows FAQ rich results for very few sites now, but the markup still makes the question-and-answer structure explicit. Our guide to [schema markup for law firms](https://legalwebagency.com/schema-markup-for-law-firms/) shows how it fits with LegalService and Attorney markup.

## Step 5: Keep it current

- Review answers when laws, fees or office hours change.
- Add a question whenever intake hears the same new one three times.
- Remove questions nobody reads, based on scroll or click data.

A yearly [law firm website content audit](https://legalwebagency.com/law-firm-website-content-audit/) is a good time to do this.

## Sample questions by practice

| Practice | Questions clients really ask |
|----------|------------------------------|
| Personal injury | How long do I have to file? Who pays my medical bills now? Do I pay if we lose? |
| Family law | How long does a divorce take here? Can I move with my child? Do we both need lawyers? |
| Criminal defense | Will this go on my record? Should I talk to the police? Can the charge be reduced? |
| Estate planning | Do I need a trust or just a will? What happens if I die without a will? |
| Immigration | How long does a green card take? Can I work while my case is pending? |
